Rotation & timing badge.rotation += 45 per copy, starting at 20745 (= 225° phase), animated by the component's expressive spring. The app adds a 45°/s ambient spin on top while the card is visible; this page mirrors it through the public rotation property (toggle above). Auto demo ticks: 1000 / 500 / 100 ms, driven by our next() loop — not the component's autoplay. The 72px card badge and 32px actual-size badge share the same sequence index and target angle. The 100ms fast mode sets the public disable-animation flag (snap) to avoid a mid-morph retarget exception in the published 0.3.0 build; 500/1000ms keep the real spring.
